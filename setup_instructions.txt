Create Sync Matrix – In-World Setup Guide
=========================================

This file documents the exact in-world infrastructure you need to build to turn Create rotational power into Sync piggawatts using the Piggawatt Converter block.

1) Place the Piggawatt Converter
- Craft or grab the Piggawatt Converter from the Redstone & Gears creative tab.
- Right-click to place it so the block's face (the side you see when placing) points at the machine you want to power. By default it should face you when placed; use a wrench or pick/block placement to rotate as needed.
- The facing direction is the side that will push piggawatts out of the block.

2) Connect rotational power from Create
- Run a shaft directly into either the front or back of the block along its facing axis. Both directions are valid for mechanical input.
- Any Create generator works: a water wheel + gear train, steam engine, windmill, etc. Higher rotational speed means more piggawatts per tick (see config for the multiplier).
- You do not need stress to exceed any specific threshold—the converter simply reads current speed and scales output.

3) Wire the output to Sync machines
- Place a Sync Shell Constructor directly on the converter's facing side. The converter exposes Forge's energy capability there, so the constructor can draw piggawatts automatically.
- If you route power through cables from other mods that speak Forge Energy, ensure they connect to the converter's facing side; the other sides are intentionally blocked to avoid infinite loops.

4) Observe and tune
- Sneak with Create Goggles equipped to see a tooltip: current buffer, maximum buffer, and the configured speed → piggawatt rate.
- If the constructor starves for power, increase the mechanical speed or adjust the config values in `config/create_sync_matrix-common.toml`:
  - `converter.piggawattPerSpeed` raises or lowers the generation rate per speed unit.
  - `converter.maxBuffer` increases the internal storage if your generators are bursty.
  - `converter.maxOutputPerTick` controls how fast the buffer empties into neighboring blocks.

5) Troubleshooting tips
- No energy movement? Verify the facing side actually points at the Shell Constructor; use F3 to check block states or rotate with a wrench.
- Speed reads zero? Ensure the shaft is inline with the converter's facing axis (front/back). Side connections will not transmit rotation.
- Values feel off? The mod intentionally treats piggawatts as Forge Energy so Sync machines that consume FE will also accept the output. If your Sync build expects a different API, adjust the dependency coordinates in `build.gradle` and the capability wiring in `PiggawattConverterBlockEntity`.
